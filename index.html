Draw your design!

Rotate your design...

Use the sliders to rotate the sphere and visualize the 3D space. The blue dot shows the face of the base ballz on which your design will be placed. By default, design is on the Back of its base ballz. Adjust the sliders or press the quick buttons to rotate design to different regions.
